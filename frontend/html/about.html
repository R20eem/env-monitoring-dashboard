About Us

Building technology that helps farmers make better decisions.

🌍

What Is This?

The Environmental Monitoring Dashboard is a web application designed to help farmers — including those new to the field — monitor their crops and make informed decisions based on real-time environmental data.

🎯

Our Goal

Our goal is to empower farmers with the knowledge and tools they need to make informed decisions about their crops — from soil moisture and temperature to air quality and flood risk.

👩‍💻

Our Team

We are a team of Level 2 undergraduate students from the University of Leeds, passionate about using technology to solve real-world problems in agriculture. This project is part of a funded research initiative on community-centred environmental monitoring and international collaboration.
